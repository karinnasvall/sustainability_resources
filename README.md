# Sustainability resources
Links and useful documents on how to make bioinformatics more sustainable.

What can I do to reduce the impact of my research activities?

### Be aware of environmental issues, my actions do have an impact

References describing the evironmental impact of scientific research

[Grealey et al. 2022, Mol Biol Evol](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msac034) quantify the carbon footprint of bioinformatic tools and commonly run analyses, using the Green Algorithms calculator. It covers GWAS, RNA-seq, genome assembly, metagenomics, phylogenetics and molecular simulations. 

[Lannelongue et al. 2023, Nat Comput Sci](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43588-023-00461-y): the GREENER principles


### Measuring and monitoring the environmental impact of my activities, how large are they and where can I reduce them?

Calculating the carbon footprint of computational analyses [Green Algorithms calculator](https://calculator.green-algorithms.org)


### How can I reduce my impact? Guidelines and recommendations for sustainability in science

Ten simple rules to make your computing more environmentally sustainable [Lannelongue et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009324)

[Green DiSC](https://www.software.ac.uk/blog/announcing-green-disc-new-digital-sustainability-certification): a certification scheme giving research groups and institutions a roadmap to tackle the environmental impact of their computing, developed with the Software Sustainability Institute. It is three-tiered (bronze, silver, gold) and free to join.

Suggestions how to reduce your impact during travel and conferences,  [green pledge](https://www.iscb.org/about-iscb/green-iscb/green-pledge)
